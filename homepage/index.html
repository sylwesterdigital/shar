Local-first media sharing

Shar

Move, preview and play files locally, or send an expiring WebRTC share link/QR to someone on another Internet connection. Native clients for Apple and Android, plus a browser receiver.

Remote WebRTC sharing

Shar creates temporary one-receiver links and QR codes. Transfers prefer direct peer-to-peer WebRTC and automatically fall back to the Shar TURN relay when NAT/firewalls require it.
