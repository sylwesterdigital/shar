Security
  • AES-256-GCM application encryption in addition to WebRTC/DTLS.
  • The 256-bit content key is stored only in the URL fragment and is never sent to the Shar signaling or TURN service.
  • A separate 6-digit PIN is required and is verified with a rate-limited PBKDF2 proof.
  • The sender must approve the receiver before TURN credentials or the WebRTC session are released.
  • Every received file is verified with SHA-256 before completion.
  • Shares are one-receiver and expire automatically.
